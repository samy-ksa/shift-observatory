About SHIFT Observatory

SHIFT Observatory is a free AI job risk dashboard for Saudi Arabia. It scores 237 occupations using the Frey and Osborne automation probability framework combined with LLM exposure data from Eloundou et al. Each occupation includes entry, median, and senior salary benchmarks in SAR, Nitaqat saudization band classification, WEF growth trends, and career transition recommendations. The relocation calculator compares cost of living across 17 origin cities including Paris, London, Cairo, Mumbai, and Manila against Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. Built for expats evaluating Saudi Arabia as a career destination under Vision 2030.
